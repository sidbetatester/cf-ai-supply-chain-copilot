---
description: Playbook of mitigation options for late or at-risk hardware deliveries
---

For each late or at-risk gating delivery, propose the two or three most relevant mitigations, each with its trade-off (cost, risk, days recovered):

- **Expedite**: air freight instead of ocean; ask the supplier to pull in the ship date. Recovers days to weeks; adds freight cost.
- **Split shipment**: ship the partial quantity needed for the first work (e.g. enough switches to start turn-up) and the remainder later.
- **Customs pre-clearance**: file import paperwork and duties before arrival; use a broker with a local presence. Recovers part of the customs buffer.
- **Alternate supplier or stock**: source the item from another approved supplier, a distributor, or spare inventory at another site.
- **Re-sequence work**: start work that does not depend on the late item (e.g. rack and cable servers before switches arrive) so the milestone slips less than the delivery.
- **Re-baseline**: if nothing recovers the gap, propose a new milestone date for the user to approve.

Recommend one option and say what decision or approval it needs, and from whom.
